## 2. Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) Calculation Formulas

### Standard Cumulative FCR:
$$\text{FCR} = \frac{\text{Total Feed Consumed by Flock (kg)}}{\text{Total Live Weight Produced (kg)} - \text{Total Starting Chick Weight (kg)}}$$

### Adjusted FCR to 2.0 kg Body Weight:
$$\text{Adjusted FCR} = \text{Actual FCR} - \frac{\text{Actual Body Weight (kg)} - 2.0\text{ kg}}{0.05 \times 10}$$

## 3. Diagnosing Root Causes for Poor Weight Gain / High FCR
1. **Feed Spillage & Feeder Height:** Feed pan lip must be level with the birds' backs. Overfilled pans cause 5% to 12% hidden feed wastage in the litter.
2. **Subclinical Enteritis / Coccidiosis:** Gut inflammation prevents nutrient absorption, causing elevated FCR even with high feed consumption.
3. **Low Brooding Temperatures:** Young chicks burn dietary energy to keep warm instead of depositing muscle tissue.
4. **Water Restraint:** Water-to-feed consumption ratio should be 1.8:1 to 2.0:1 at 21°C. If water lines are blocked or nipple flow rate is below standard (60–90 ml/min), feed intake will drop immediately.
5. **Feed Quality & Mycotoxins:** Check for high moisture, rancid fats, or low digestible crude protein/amino acid profiles.
