# Veterinary Compendium: Pharmacology, Medication Safety & Withdrawal Standards

## 1. Antimicrobial Stewardship & Safety Imperatives
- **Strict Prohibition on Blind / Prophylactic Use:** Antibiotics must NEVER be administered routinely as growth promoters or preventative crutches. Use only for targeted therapeutic treatment of laboratory-confirmed or veterinarian-diagnosed bacterial infections.
- **Viral Non-Response:** Antibiotics are completely ineffective against viruses (Newcastle, Gumboro, Infectious Bronchitis, Avian Influenza). Prescribing antibiotics for pure viral infections promotes dangerous drug-resistant pathogens.

## 2. Scientific Principles for Medication Dosage Calculation
- **Flock Dosage Calculation Formula (Active Ingredient Principle):**
  $$\text{Daily Product Needed (grams or ml)} = \frac{\text{Number of Live Birds} \times \text{Average Live Weight (kg)} \times \text{Prescribed Dose Rate (mg/kg)}}{\text{Active Ingredient Concentration of Product (mg/g or mg/ml)}}$$
- **Water Dilution Volume:**
  - Dilute the daily calculated product dose into the volume of drinking water consumed by the flock in **4 to 6 hours during morning hours**.
  - Treated water must be consumed fresh within this window to avoid photochemical degradation of active antibiotic molecules.

## 3. Mandatory Meat Withdrawal Periods Prior to Slaughter

| Therapeutic Class | Active Ingredient | Standard Indication | Minimum Mandatory Withdrawal Period |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Fluoroquinolones** | Enrofloxacin / Norfloxacin | Severe *E. coli* Colibacillosis, Coryza | **7 to 10 Days** |
| **Tetracyclines** | Doxycycline / Oxytetracycline | CRD, Synovitis, Ornithobacterium | **5 to 7 Days** |
| **Macrolides** | Tylosin / Tilmicosin | *Mycoplasma* Chronic Respiratory Disease | **1 to 3 Days** |
| **Anticoccidials** | Toltrazuril (2.5% solution) | Acute Coccidiosis | **14 to 16 Days** |
| **Anticoccidials** | Amprolium (20% powder) | Clinical & Subclinical Coccidiosis | **3 to 5 Days** |
| **Sulfonamides** | Sulfaclozine / Sulfamethoxazole | Coccidiosis + Fowl Cholera | **7 to 10 Days** |

## 4. Emergency Clinical Situations Requiring Immediate Licensed Veterinarian Intervention
The AI assistant and farm personnel MUST escalate to a licensed poultry veterinarian immediately under any of the following critical triggers:
1. **Acute Mortality Spike:** Daily mortality exceeding **1.0% of the flock in a 24-hour window** (e.g. > 20 deaths in 2,000 birds) or rising mortality trend over 3 consecutive days.
2. **Neurological Symptoms:** Birds exhibiting torticollis (twisted neck), stargazing, circling, or flaccid paralysis of legs and wings (suspected Newcastle Disease or Marek's Disease).
3. **Severe Hemorrhagic Enteritis:** Copious frank blood in droppings with severe flock prostration (suspected acute *Eimeria tenella* cecal coccidiosis).
4. **Suspected Highly Pathogenic Zoonoses:** Sudden mass death with cyanotic combs/wattles and subcutaneous leg hemorrhages (suspected Avian Influenza).
