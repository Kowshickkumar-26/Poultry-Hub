## 2. Mandatory Rules for Water-Administered Vaccines
1. **Chlorine & Heavy Metal Neutralization:**
   - Chlorine and tap sanitizers instantly kill live attenuated vaccine viruses.
   - Mix **Skimmed Milk Powder (2.0 grams per liter)** or a specialized vaccine dye tablet into the water tank 20 minutes before adding vaccine vials.
2. **Water Thirst Deprivation (Pre-Vaccination):**
   - Withdraw drinking water for **1.5 to 2.0 hours** in warm climates (2.0 to 2.5 hours in cool weather) prior to vaccination so birds drink aggressively.
3. **Consumption Window:**
   - The entire volume of vaccinated water must be consumed by the flock within **90 to 120 minutes** (2 hours maximum).
4. **Cold Chain Maintenance:**
   - Store all live vaccines between **2°C and 8°C (35°F – 46°F)** in a monitored refrigerator. Never expose to direct sunlight or warm ambient shed air before dilution.
5. **No Antibiotics / Water Sanitizers:**
   - Discontinue all water acidifiers, chlorine, hydrogen peroxide, and antibiotics 24 hours before and 24 hours after live vaccination.
